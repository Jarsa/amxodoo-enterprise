When a bank statement line in a foreign currency (for example USD) is
reconciled against customer invoices issued in the company currency
(MXN), the bank reconciliation widget keeps the receivable counterpart
in MXN inside a USD journal entry and leaves the exchange difference to
be booked as a write-off. The standard CFDI payment complement then
reads the reconciled MXN amount as if it were USD and stamps
``MonedaP="USD"``, ``TipoCambioP="1"``, ``Monto`` equal to the MXN
amount and the tax totals of the whole invoice.

This module takes the amount and the exchange rate from the bank
liquidity line instead, so the complement reports the USD actually
received, the real ``TipoCambioP``, the ``EquivalenciaDR`` implied by
the reconciled amount and tax totals (``TotalTrasladosImpuestoIVA16``,
``MontoTotalPagos``) expressed at the real rate, as required by the SAT
filling guide for Pagos 2.0.

Usage
=====

No configuration is needed. Reconcile the foreign currency statement
line with the MXN invoices from the bank reconciliation widget, book the
remaining difference with a write-off (manual line or reconciliation
model) and stamp the payment complement as usual.
