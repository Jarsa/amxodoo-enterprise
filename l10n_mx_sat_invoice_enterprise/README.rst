This module adapts ``l10n_mx_sat_invoice`` to the Enterprise Mexican
localization (``l10n_mx_edi``).

When an invoice is created from a CFDI downloaded from the SAT:

- CFDIs whose fiscal folio is already registered in an invoice are
  rejected, and the status of the fiscal folio is validated against the
  SAT: cancelled or unknown CFDIs are rejected and the SAT document is
  updated.
- The invoice gets its ``l10n_mx_edi.document`` in the *Sent* state
  (issued CFDIs, so Odoo does not stamp them again) or *Received* state
  (received CFDIs) with the XML, the fiscal folio and the SAT status.
- The payment way, payment policy, usage and related CFDIs (origin) of
  the invoice are taken from the CFDI.

Usage
=====

Use **Create Invoice** on the SAT documents as described in
``l10n_mx_sat_invoice``. The created invoices show the fiscal folio and
the SAT status in the **MX EDI** section.

**Link SAT CFDI** on an existing invoice also registers the fiscal folio
of the chosen CFDI. An invoice that already has a fiscal folio (stamped
in Odoo or received) can only be linked to the CFDI of that folio, which
is proposed by the wizard.

Bank reconciliation
-------------------

In the bank reconciliation, the menu (⋮) of a transaction has the option
**Create from SAT CFDIs**, next to **Upload Bills**:

1. The wizard takes the direction from the transaction: money out pays
   vendor bills (received CFDIs), money in pays customer invoices
   (issued CFDIs).
2. Add the CFDIs paid by the transaction. Only invoices (type I) without
   invoice in Odoo are listed, filtered by the RFC of the partner of the
   transaction; uncheck **Only CFDIs of the partner** to list all of
   them. The total of the selected CFDIs and the difference with the
   transaction are shown below the list.
3. Check the journal and the account and click **Create and Match**: the
   invoices are created as with **Create Invoice** and matched with the
   transaction. When the CFDIs add up to more than the transaction, the
   last one is left partially paid.

Automatic link by fiscal folio
------------------------------

An invoice that has a fiscal folio (stamped in Odoo or received with its
XML) is linked to the downloaded CFDI of that folio without any action:
when the CFDI is downloaded from the SAT and when the invoice gets its
folio. The **Link SAT CFDI** button is hidden on those invoices; it is
meant for the invoices without fiscal folio. Updating the module links
the existing invoices.
