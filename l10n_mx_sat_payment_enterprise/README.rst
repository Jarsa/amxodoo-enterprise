Bridge between ``l10n_mx_sat_payment`` and ``l10n_mx_edi``: the payments
applied from the SAT payment complements get the payment method of the
complement and, for issued complements, the stamped payment CFDI as
their EDI document, so Odoo does not stamp a second payment complement
for them. The invoices paid by a complement are also found by their
fiscal folio.

.. IMPORTANT::
   This is an alpha version, the data model and design can change at any time without warning.
   Only for development or testing purpose, do not use in production.
   `More details on development status <https://odoo-community.org/page/development-status>`_
